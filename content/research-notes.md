# Participation source review

Checked 6 November 2026, Australia/Brisbane. This is a dated research snapshot. Rebuilding pages does not refresh sources.

The founder’s place is the starting path. Minjerribah / North Stradbroke Island, Quandamooka governance, community activity and Redland City Council are described on their own terms. Council participation requires its actual rules. Requests to address a Redland meeting require approval; councillor question and remote-attendance provisions are not represented as public entitlements. Watching a livestream does not create a speaking right.

The Redland Your Say audit examined 114 project pages. Many recently updated cards link to old closed consultations. The budget, animal-law and new City Plan preliminary-feedback invitations are distinguished from past Minjerribah planning and ferry consultations. Community coverage remains selective.

The federal accepting-submissions index showed 13 inquiries. All 13 individual pages were checked. Twelve have consistent open invitations. Women’s Health remains uncertain because the index gives 31 January 2027 and its detail page gives 9 October 2026. Both sources still signal acceptance. Both dates are retained as a conflict and neither is invented as a confirmed cutoff. Additional specialist-care survey and Perth hearing-registration routes are distinct records. Postal services expressly gives 5 pm Canberra time on 7 December; other date-only deadlines keep no invented time.

Queensland’s 58-row current-inquiry table includes institutional oversight and expired submission windows. Three actual parliamentary invitations were checked in detail. The main text of all 47 undated inquiry pages was also checked and contained no additional public invitation. Linked attachments and later invitations remain possible gaps. The non-fatal strangulation Bill invites submissions but publishes no closing date. Its 29 April 2027 reporting date is not used as a submission deadline. The Rural and Regional Adjustment Bill’s timeline gives 10 am on 20 November; the intimate-image Bill gives 4 pm on 9 November. Their pages do not explicitly state a timezone.

NSW’s two open index entries and WA’s two were enumerated. ACT’s seven dated calls were supplemented by a separately open Economics annual-report inquiry and six upcoming annual hearing programmes. Tasmania’s directories and 50 source pages yielded two open written calls and three upcoming hearing or scrutiny programmes; unclear ongoing invitations remain a gap. Victoria’s committees ceased and unfinished inquiries lapsed at Parliament expiry on 3 November. Its Sentencing Advisory Council’s future consultation is a separate government process.

South Australia’s 19 current committees were identified. Official news and inquiry material verify the energy and foster/kinship invitations, older closed calls, and a separate AI Royal Commission consultation. The full attachment-based committee inventory remains partial; Men’s Health is uncertain. NT’s visible index records were reported or suspended, and no open invitation was verified. That is not proof that none exists.

Only short original summaries, official links and evidence notes are published. Raw research pages, participant lists, screenshots and browser material stay outside the source repository and Pages artifact.

A closed record moves to that jurisdiction’s archive when its canonical status is updated after verification. Its original detail URL and source dates remain stable. Keep outcomes separate from intentions, and use only verified outcome statements.
