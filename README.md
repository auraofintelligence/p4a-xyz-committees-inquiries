# P4A committees and inquiries

[Open this P4A section](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/p4a-xyz-committees-inquiries/) · [Main P4A workbench](https://p4a.xyz/)

This is the public-participation research workspace of P4A the Purple Party for Australia, held in a separate repository because the main Pages site has a conservative size budget. It uses the existing P4A theme, branding, six primary navigation doors, searchable five-theme Index, footer and site-map pattern. Existing Twinkles are linked where a real topic match exists and are not edited.

P4A’s founder and designer lives on North Stradbroke Island / Minjerribah. The starting path follows the community and government relationships affecting his life: Redland City, Queensland and the Commonwealth. Other states and territories are reached through navigation.

Visitors choose a responsible body before searching. Australia shows federal participation only; each state shows state-level participation. Each parliament has separate Inquiries & participation and All committees views. Council participation has its own state/territory chooser and individual council pages, while Minjerribah and Redland retain their dedicated starting path. There is no combined opportunity view. Every current record is visible by default, including uncertain invitations with an explicit tag and explanation. Closed contribution rounds retain their detail URLs and move to that parliament or council's own archive.

The section menu offers Inquiries & participation, All committees and Parliament calendars. Changing the view retains the selected parliament. The Council participation shortcut opens a separate state or territory chooser with a page for each council. Homelands connects the Redland City, Queensland and Australian views. Redland's local committee evidence is in `data/redland-committees.json`. Its participation and archive have their own views.

The searchable Index and the human site map both link to Parliament calendars. Each parliament has a schedule for chamber sittings and committee hearings with official programs, downloads where verified, and a separate check date. Update verified dates and source links in `data/parliament-calendars.json`. Mark reserve or optional sitting days as provisional. Calendar highlights use the parliament's local date.

## Research-to-participation workflow

Choose the responsible body first, then the specific invitation. Open its detail page to check who may contribute, the inquiry purpose or terms of reference, official submission route, method, actual closing date/time and timezone, publication/privacy conditions and source date. If a method is restricted, closed or uncertain, keep that distinction rather than replacing it with a generic open label. People submit independently through the official route; the directory does not lodge responses or send personal information.

Where a real connection exists, the record explains the relevant unchanged P4A Twinkle or system. Priority does not exclude unrelated opportunities and is not an endorsement. The separate [P4A membership workspace](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/p4a-membership/) can support drafting, collaborative project work and voluntary contribution review, but there is no automatic synchronisation, shared applicant database or submission integration.

For scoped maintenance, record new invitations, deadline/method changes, hearings, explicit closure and any relevant source-access limitations. Preserve stable record IDs, exact original-source links and old dates. Keep official committee lists, actual accepting-submission counts and council inventories separate. A no-change check is not a whole-directory refresh.

## Maintain and preview

Read [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) and [research notes](content/research-notes.md).

```text
python tools/build.py
python tools/validate.py
python -m http.server 8772 --bind 127.0.0.1 --directory _site
```

Open `http://127.0.0.1:8772/` in Chrome. The generated `_site/` directory is ignored by Git. The Pages workflow builds and validates it, then uploads only the checked public artifact. No screenshots, browser profiles, dependencies or raw source-page downloads are published.

After an official check confirms that a round closed, change that record’s status to `closed`, retain its dates and source links, add any verified outcome separately, and rebuild. The generator moves it to its own jurisdiction’s archive while preserving its detail page. A reporting date or an old cached call is not a submission deadline. Research dates do not advance on rebuild.

There must be exactly zero em dashes in source and generated files. Validation rejects literal and encoded forms. The P4A stylesheet has one comment punctuation change and keeps the same visual rules.
